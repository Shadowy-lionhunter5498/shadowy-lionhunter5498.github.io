🔧 Troubleshooting Tips

Encountering a hiccup? Try these quick fixes:

  • 🔄 Can't see the 3D model? Make sure your Windows graphics drivers are up to date. Search "Device Manager" on your PC, right-click your display adapter, and select "Update driver."
  • 🛡️ SmartScreen warning? If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" screen, click "More info" and then "Run anyway." This is a standard warning for new downloads.
  • 📉 The app is laggy? Try closing other heavy programs (like browsers with many tabs) while using the app.
  • 🗑️ Installation fails? Restart your computer, then try the download again. Make sure you have enough free disk space (at least 500 MB).
